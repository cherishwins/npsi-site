# NPSI Korean Glossary

Canonical Korean renderings for NPSI working papers and LinkedIn captions. Consult before delivering any EN ↔ KO translation. Items marked **[VERIFY]** are best-effort translations from non-primary sources — confirm against the institution's own Korean website, ROK government publication, or other primary source before publication.

If a term is needed and not in this file, flag it in the translator notes rather than guessing.

## Slur-adjacent and politically loaded vocabulary

**Never produce in NPSI voice.** Permissible only inside attributed direct quotes from a source.

- 북괴 (slur for North Korea — "North puppet")
- 빨갱이 (slur, "red")
- 종북 (politicized — "follows the North")
- 남조선 괴뢰 (DPRK-side slur for ROK — "South Korean puppet regime")
- 미제 / 미제국주의자 (DPRK-side term for US — "American imperialists"). Acceptable inside quoted DPRK source; do not import into NPSI voice.
- 김씨일가 / 김씨 왕조 (loaded — "Kim family / Kim dynasty")

## DPRK orthographic markers — preserve when quoting DPRK sources

These are the most common ROK-vs-DPRK orthographic differences. When quoting a DPRK source, preserve the DPRK form; do not normalize to ROK form.

| ROK form | DPRK form | English |
|---|---|---|
| 노동 | 로동 | labor / work |
| 노동신문 | 로동신문 | Rodong Sinmun (newspaper) |
| 역사 | 력사 | history |
| 여성 | 녀성 | women |
| 이론 | 리론 | theory |
| 이용 | 리용 | use |
| 양심 | 량심 | conscience |
| 영도 | 령도 | leadership (DPRK political term) |
| 노인 | 로인 | elderly person |
| 낙원 | 락원 | paradise |
| 양식 | 량식 | provisions / form |

The pattern: DPRK preserves initial ㄹ where ROK changed it to ㄴ; DPRK preserves initial ㄴ before ㅣ/ㅑ/ㅕ/ㅛ/ㅠ where ROK dropped it. Recognize the pattern; do not "correct" it in DPRK quotations.

## Verification protocol

Items flagged **[VERIFY]** should be confirmed before any external publication. Sources of record, in priority order:

1. The institution's own Korean-language website
2. ROK government primary publication (MOFA, MOTIE, MND white papers, National Assembly research service)
3. Korean institutional finance press (매일경제, 한국경제)
4. Major Korean daily editorial (조선일보, 중앙일보, 한겨레)
5. Korean Wikipedia (last resort; double-check)

If verification is not possible in the time available, mark in the translator notes that the term is best-effort and recommend Jesse confirm before final publication.
