## Special Briefing No. 4 — The Tollgate Markets (`sb4/index.html`)

Market-enforcement briefing, integrated 4 October 2026 as a **PDF-first release page**. Key facts that should remain consistent:

- **Identity:** the document ID is the one printed on the release, `NPSI-20261003-B` (not `NPSI-SB-004`); on this site it is Special Briefing No. 4. v1.0 was issued 3 October 2026 and circulated on LinkedIn; v1.0.1 of 4 October 2026, corrected after a source check, is the release hosted here. The analysis and the ladder's ratings did not change between them.
- **Turkey, the case:** 131 funds ordered into liquidation on 17 September 2026 (SPK Bulletins 2026/60 and 2026/61); 455,758 investors (MKK, via Reuters); Tera Portföy's TP2 held TRY 224.4bn on 16 September; fund-size estimates run from $16.5bn to more than $20bn by source and date. By 30 September prosecutors had named 217 suspects and jailed 56; the justice minister spoke of "Ponzi-like methods." No court had ruled on guilt as of 4 October 2026: report every allegation as an allegation.
- **The price list:** in 2025 the SPK fined 297 people and companies TRY 2.69bn (about $65–70m), roughly 1% of TP2. **Article 107(3)** removes the penalty only if twice the benefit (at least TRY 500,000) is paid *before an investigation opens*; later payment halves the sentence or cuts it by a third; the 15 days is the SPK's deadline in its 2026 complaints. Never write that payment within 15 days closes the case. Art. 107/1 prison range: three to five years since Law 7222 (2020).
- **The term:** "A tollgate market is a market where breaking the rules is a toll you pay, not a wall you hit."
- **Three weaknesses, with their examples:** the gain nobody measures (Vietnam: VND 1.5bn, about $57,000, is the *fixed fine when no gain is established*, not a ceiling; otherwise ten times the gain); the fine nobody collects (Canada's provincial regulators collected about 35.7% of penalties imposed **from 2007 to 2012**, British Columbia 2.9%); the trial that never comes (Indonesia's OJK, 9 February 2026: 42 suspected criminal cases under investigation, 32 of them manipulation, and five concluded cases with final verdicts — not "five verdicts in 42 cases").
- **The ladder:** seventeen markets, softest to strongest, rated on enforcement in practice: Turkey (pre-September 2026), Vietnam, Bangladesh (provisional, under review), Indonesia, Thailand, Canada, Brazil, Japan, India, South Korea, China, EU/Germany, United Kingdom, Hong Kong, Australia, Singapore, United States. Fifteen further markets are unrated; absence is not clearance. The ratings are an assessment, not a measurement.
- **Where it matters now:** Indonesia and Vietnam most resemble pre-collapse Turkey, Thailand close behind, Bangladesh a provisional fourth. Severe sentences arrive after collapses: 40 years in Korea, 36 in Singapore, life in Indonesia. Korea: Lee Jong-pil's sentence is **20 years**, not 15.
- **Withdrawn line, never restore:** an Indonesian OJK supervisor was convicted of corruption and **acquitted by the Supreme Court in 2022**; the first edition's claim that he drew 8 years is withdrawn.
- **The Tollgate Test:** five signs (thin floats and fresh listings; funds that own each other's trades; "cash" that holds stock; promoters close to power; a price on prosecution) and four numbers to demand (collection rate, median fine, referrals and convictions, pay-to-close route). Three or more signs, and the penalty code is the wrong document to read.
- **Register:** the briefing's "From the author" note (p. 20) is signed and in the first person; that is the author's form for this document. AI assistance is stated on the back cover and in the page's metadata block.
