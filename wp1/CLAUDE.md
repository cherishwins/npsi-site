## Working Paper No. 1 — A Canada–Korea Pacific Infrastructure Facility (`wp1/index.html`)

Proposes the CKPIF — a treaty-based supranational issuer for Pacific corridor infrastructure. Key facts that should remain consistent across any future edits:

- 50/50 Canada–Korea ownership, Luxembourg seat, English law, LCIA arbitration
- Indicative programme size US$25–40 billion over 5–7 years
- Indicative pricing +50–80 bp over 30Y UST at launch
- Several (not joint) sovereign guarantees
- Indigenous Series I tranche, 10–15% of programme, +25 bp ratchet protection
- KPI architecture follows ICMA Sustainability-Linked Bond Principles, 25 bp step calibration
- Tokenised pilot tranche US$200–500 m on Project Agorá-class infrastructure (BoK is a participant)
- BoC–BoK standing swap line, signed 15 November 2017, no expiry, no preset limit — the institutional foundation; Canada's only standing swap arrangement outside the six-central-bank network made permanent on 31 October 2013 (Fed, ECB, BoE, BoJ, SNB, BoC)
- HBM share: SK Hynix and Samsung together produce *more than 75 percent* of global HBM (Counterpoint Q3 2025 estimate: ~53% / ~35%) — never "~90%"
- Hanwha Ocean–Algoma Steel MOU: signed 26 January 2026 (not "late 2025"), USD 250 m / CAD 345 m, binding but conditional on a CPSP award to Hanwha Ocean; suspended by Algoma on 29 July 2026 "unless and until Hanwha Ocean is selected" (see `wp3/CLAUDE.md`)
- Korea's oil: about 70 percent of supplies from the Middle East in 2025 (Industry Ministry data), most via the Strait of Hormuz — never "95% of crude through the Strait"
- 2012 Greek debt exchange: ISDA declared a restructuring credit event on 9 March 2012; it did *not* avoid triggering CDS
- Staging: CKPIF Phase 1 launches on less contested first projects with the pipeline sequenced behind them; WP2's compact projects are Phase 2 (settled 14 Oct 2026 with WP2's canon)
- The 30-year spread ladder is indicative, stated on a USD-swapped basis; it is not a statement of local-currency yields
- Author: Jesse James (editor). **Two editions under one number (settled 14 October 2026):** the reading view is the *technical specification*, written from an April 2026 vantage, live on 25 April 2026 under the original title and redated to May on 17 May; the PDF is the *essay edition* of 15 May 2026, the author's typeset document, which the home card summarises. The paper's publication date for citation and the register stays **May 2026**. The essay edition is not re-typeset; its known errors (the swap line's age — eight and a half years, not twelve; the C6 standing swap network since 2013; press treatment of the 23 May Esquimalt visit in a document dated 15 May; WP2/WP3 listed as in development; first person) are declared in a dated note on the page.
- **v1.0.1 (14 October 2026):** errata to the reading view per the editor's review docket (WP1-1, WP1-3, WP1-4, WP1-5; WP1-2 by on-page note): Greek credit event, units ("1 million b/d"), programme size US$25–40 bn, §7 and §11 language, Algoma date, HBM share, Hormuz figure, ladder basis, Sources section added.
