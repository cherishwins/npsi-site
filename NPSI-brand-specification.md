# North Pacific Strategy Initiative — Brand Specification

**Internal reference. Not for distribution.**
v1.0  ·  April 2026  ·  Editor: Jesse James

---

## 1. Strategic positioning

NPSI is an **independent research imprint**. Editorial register is institutional, restrained, treaty-document quality. The visual register is *Brookings, Foreign Affairs, IISS, CIGI, RAND* — never *Forbes, Substack, consultancy, advocacy, movement*. Every visual and written choice should reinforce that the work is serious enough to be cited and quoted by senior policy readers without any element of the presentation undermining its credibility.

The brand exists to support placement of NPSI working papers with senior fiduciary, diplomatic, and policy readers in Canada, Korea, and adjacent Pacific jurisdictions. It is not a movement and not a personal platform. The author signs the work; the imprint hosts it.

---

## 2. Name and presentation

**Full name.** North Pacific Strategy Initiative
**Short form.** NPSI
**First-mention usage.** "the North Pacific Strategy Initiative (NPSI)" — full name first, parenthetical short form, then NPSI thereafter.
**Never.** "Pacific Strategy," "Pacific Initiative," lowercase "npsi" except in URLs and email addresses.

**Subordinate descriptor.** "Working Papers on Pacific Sovereignty & Bilateral Architecture" — used in wordmark lockups and on the masthead of the imprint website. Not used in body copy.

**Volume identifier.** "Vol. I · Est. MMXXVI" — appears on full wordmark and on the colophon of the website. Roman numerals are intentional; they signal the imprint's institutional posture.

## 3. Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Pacific Navy | `#0E2B47` | Wordmark, document headings, masthead, dominant ink |
| Paper | Document Cream | `#F4EFE3` | Backgrounds; reads as printed working paper, not slide deck |
| Accent | Treaty Bronze | `#A47148` | Meridian rules, italic descriptors, KPI numbers, accent borders |
| Secondary | Maritime Teal | `#3D6A78` | Section markers, classification lines, monospace metadata |
| Body | Charcoal | `#1A1A1A` | Body text on cream; never use pure black |
| Rule | Bone | `#C4B79B` | Dashed and thin rules between sections |

**Restrictions.**
- Never use any red. Both flags use red, and using it conflates the imprint with national branding.
- Never use any pure white background; cream is the paper.
- Bronze is a thin accent, never a fill. Maximum 5% of any composition.
- Charts use Navy as primary series, Bronze as secondary, Teal as tertiary, in that order.

## 4. Typography

**Display & headers.** Source Serif 4 (or Source Serif Pro fallback). Weight 600 for headers, 700 for the main document title. Generous letter-spacing on small caps (0.14–0.20em).

**Body.** Source Sans 3 in web/screen contexts; Source Serif 4 in print/document contexts. 10.5–11pt body in print; 16–18px in web. Line-height 1.4–1.5.

**Korean script.** Noto Serif KR for headers; Noto Sans KR for body. Pair natively with Source Serif 4 / Source Sans 3.

**Monospace.** JetBrains Mono (or IBM Plex Mono fallback). Used for: classification lines, page numbers, KPI numbers, document metadata, the volume identifier, and any displayed code.

**Italic for emphasis.** Italic in Source Serif 4 carries definitions (a *defensive* not *offensive* architecture) and pull quotes. Bronze italic for descriptors and pull-quote text. Never use bold for emphasis in body copy; bold is for proper nouns and section titles only.

**Never use.** Inter, Roboto, Arial, system-default sans-serif fonts. Generic AI aesthetic; immediately reads as not-quite-serious.

## 5. Document chrome

Every NPSI document — Working Paper, Executive Brief, transmittal letter, Figure — carries the same chrome:

**Top of every page.**
- Left: small-caps wordmark `NORTH PACIFIC STRATEGY INITIATIVE` in Pacific Navy at 9–10pt.
- Right: monospace metadata in Maritime Teal at 8pt — `WORKING PAPER NO. X · DOCUMENT TYPE · PAGE OF TOTAL`.
- Below both: a thin Treaty Bronze rule (0.5–0.75pt).

**Document classification line** (top of first page).
- Format: `FOR DISCUSSION  ·  v1.0  ·  APRIL 2026`
- Set in JetBrains Mono, Treaty Bronze, 8pt, generous letter-spacing.

**Footer of every page.**
- Format: `NORTH PACIFIC STRATEGY INITIATIVE  ·  [page number]  ·  [doc type]  ·  [doc-id]  ·  v[version]`
- Set in JetBrains Mono, Maritime Teal, 7.5–8pt.
- Above it: thin Bone rule (0.4pt).

**Margins.** Generous. 0.85" left/right minimum on letter-sized documents; more is better for reference-grade work. 0.75" top/bottom.

**Page numbers.** Every page. Mono, teal, footer-right.

## 6. Wordmark and lockup

The full wordmark is the official mark of the imprint and appears on:
- Document covers
- Title page of the Working Paper
- Top of the website
- Email signature blocks (if used as image)

The compact masthead — wordmark with thin bronze rule — appears on:
- Top of every page of every document
- Header of every page on the website
- Top of every released figure

**Construction of the wordmark.**
- Three small ticks on the meridian rule represent Victoria — mid-Pacific — Busan. Conceptual, not literal.
- Wordmark text is Source Serif 4, weight 600, letter-spacing 0.14em.
- Italic descriptor ("Working Papers on Pacific Sovereignty & Bilateral Architecture") in Treaty Bronze.
- Volume marker ("VOL. I · EST. MMXXVI") in JetBrains Mono, Maritime Teal.

**Usage rules.**
- Never recolor the wordmark. Pacific Navy on Document Cream only.
- Never stretch or alter the proportions.
- Maintain a clear-space margin equal to the height of the wordmark on all four sides.
- Never combine with national flags, national symbols, or national colors.
- Never use a symbol, mascot, or icon. The wordmark is the mark.

## 7. Editorial voice

**Tone.** Analytical, direct, sober. Sentences carry their weight. No adverbial inflation. No exclamation marks. No epithets toward any government.

**First person.** Avoided in body text of working papers. Used sparingly in transmittal letters where the editor is signing personally.

**Hedging.** Genuine uncertainty is named honestly: "indicative," "approximate," "subject to," "where the evidence is contested." Avoid the false-precision register of consultancy decks. Avoid the false-modesty register of academic prose.

**Numbers.** Presented in JetBrains Mono in tables and in pull-out fields; in regular Source Serif in flowing prose. Always cited.

**The "Korea" convention.** In body text of working papers, "Korea" refers to the geographic and civilizational entity in the convention of academic literature. In matters of protocol — transmittal letters to the Government of the Republic of Korea, formal invitations, official correspondence — the formal name "Republic of Korea" is used throughout. This distinction is intentional and is preserved in every document.

**Anti-American framing.** Never. The imprint takes no position critical of the United States. The thesis is *counterparty-risk diversification* and *additive financial architecture*, not antagonism.

**Sources.** Every factual claim has a citation. Web sources include URL. Citations follow standard policy-paper convention; no need for Chicago or APA strictness, but consistency within a document is required.

## 8. Web presence

**Domain.** `npsi.ca` is the canonical domain, registered for ten years. The choice of `.ca` is strategic, not a fallback:

- The `.ca` registry (CIRA) requires verifiable Canadian presence, which blocks typosquatters and copycats by registry policy in addition to ownership.
- `.ca` signals Canadian rooting honestly. The imprint's editorial seat is in Victoria, British Columbia. The wordmark and content do the bilateral Canada–Korea framing; the domain is the address.
- A ten-year registration is itself a permanence signal that distinguishes NPSI from advocacy startups on disposable `.com` and `.io` domains.

**Defensive portfolio (optional).** If desired and budget permits, register `npsi.org` (US$12/yr) as a redirect to `npsi.ca`, and `northpacific.org` if available, also as a redirect. These are insurance against confusion, not active identities. The canonical citation form is and remains `npsi.ca`.

**Site structure.** Multi-page institutional site at `/` with: home with imprint statement and current Working Paper card, working-paper reading view at `/wp[N]`, About, Engage, Commentary index, Colophon, and 404 — all in NPSI document chrome. No marketing language. No "subscribe to learn more."

**Working paper publication.** Each Working Paper has a permanent URL of the form `npsi.ca/wp[N]` where N is the working paper number. v1.0 lives at `npsi.ca/wp1`. Subsequent versions update the same URL; release-pinned URLs (`/wp1/v1.0`) are also available.

**GitHub.** Each Working Paper has a corresponding repository at `github.com/npsi-pacific/working-paper-[N]`. The repository carries the Working Paper text, the Executive Brief, the architecture figures, and the named commentary directory. The GitHub organisation name is held separately from the domain — `npsi-pacific` reads cleanly as a GitHub handle and decouples the platform identity from the domain choice.

**Email.** `editor@npsi.ca` for editorial; `commentary@npsi.ca` for named commentary submissions; `press@npsi.ca` for press inquiries (initially forwarded to `editor@`). All three configured via Cloudflare Email Routing or registrar email forwarding to a single inbox.

**Citation form.** Working papers are cited with the canonical URL: `https://npsi.ca/wp[N]/`. The shorter form `npsi.ca/wp1` is acceptable in body text and footnotes; the full `https://` form is required in formal bibliographic citation.

## 9. Social presence

NPSI does not have its own social presence. The imprint is institutional and the brand discipline is to be quoted, not to broadcast. The author (Jesse James) may post excerpts and links from his personal account on LinkedIn, in his Sutter voice; substantive comments on those posts are invited to the Named Commentary channel through `commentary@npsi.ca` rather than answered on the platform.

This is intentional. Think tanks that cultivate large social followings convert into advocacy organizations within five years. NPSI is not an advocacy organization.

---

## 10. Publishing cadence

Volume I publishes Working Papers as substantive material is ready. There is no mandatory cadence. The imprint's quality bar is a single, rigorously-sourced, reference-grade document published occasionally — not a content stream.

Every Working Paper is identified by:
- A Working Paper number (`No. 1`, `No. 2`, …)
- A version (`v1.0`, `v1.1`, `v2.0`)
- A release date
- A document ID (`NPSI-WP-001`)

Every release is tagged in the corresponding GitHub repository and appears on the imprint website with full release-version metadata.
